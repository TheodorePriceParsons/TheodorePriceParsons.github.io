How it works

Overview

ENPH 253 (Robot Summer) is UBC Engineering Physics' competitive robotics course. Our robot used a mecanum drive and two ESP32-S3s. I owned the sensor ESP firmware, written in ESP-IDF.

What I built

  • IR beacon detection at 1 kHz and 10 kHz using the Goertzel algorithm on continuous ADC DMA.
  • Metal detection with a Colpitts oscillator whose frequency is counted by the PCNT peripheral.
  • Dual PMW3901 optical flow sensors for odometry.
  • A length-prefixed, XOR-checksummed UART framing protocol between the two ESPs.
  • FreeRTOS task architecture and a WiFi PID tuning dashboard.

Debugging

A lot of the work was toolchain: pioarduino vs. official PlatformIO, native USB CDC on the S3, and a corrupted PlatformIO cache.

Next

We didn't get to use PCBs in the course, so I'm designing a KiCad sensor board for this firmware as a side exercise.
