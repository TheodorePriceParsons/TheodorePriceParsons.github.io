Phased-Array Imaging Sonar

An 8×8 ultrasonic phased array at 40 kHz for 3D imaging in air, on a DE10-Lite FPGA with a custom shield.

VerilogFPGAKiCadBeamforming

How it works

Goal

A 3D imaging sonar that works in air: an 8×8 array of 40 kHz ultrasonic transducers steered as a phased array.

Architecture

  • DE10-Lite FPGA development board for timing and beamforming, written in Verilog.
  • Custom transducer shield designed in KiCad.

Status

Schematic in progress; the LDO and board-to-board header are drawn.
