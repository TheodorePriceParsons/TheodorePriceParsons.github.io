Multi-Node CAN Bootloader

A custom bootloader to flash several STM32s at once over a CAN bus.

CANSTM32BootloaderC

How it works

Goal

Flash multiple STM32 boards at the same time over a shared CAN bus instead of plugging into each one.

Plan

  • Small-scale demo first with two nodes: a NUCLEO-G474RE and a NUCLEO-F446RE.
  • Once it works, propose it to UBC Sailbot as a prototype for flashing the boards on Zephyr, the team's next boat.
