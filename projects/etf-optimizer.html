Overview

A portfolio optimizer for ETFs, built with a friend.

Math engine

  • Constrained mean-variance optimization solved in closed form with Lagrange multipliers, falling back to SLSQP when the closed form doesn't apply.
  • Kalman filter to smooth prices before estimating returns and covariance.
  • Fixed a covariance time-scale mismatch that was skewing results.
