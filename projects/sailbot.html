UBC Sailbot: Electrical Drive

Electrical Drive Team on an autonomous sailboat: IMU firmware, GNSS debugging, rudder hardware and a sensorless FOC driver.

How it works

Overview

UBC Sailbot builds autonomous sailboats. I'm on the Electrical Drive Team.

Work

  • PLRS-IMU: RP2040 / FreeRTOS firmware with EKF sensor fusion; contributed the telemetry task split.
  • Helped debug the boat's Septentrio mosaic-H GNSS receiver.
  • Testing and troubleshooting the mast encoder, rudder motor and servo on the FDCAN bus.
  • Debugging and fixing the team's BLDC driver dev board (V1), which had a number of problems.
  • Assigned the sensorless FOC driver for a 24 V gimbal motor (up to 22 A), waiting on hardware.
